Discover sigma-form ODE from own data; c to 132 digits with no fit

Operator proposed subtracting known higher-order terms instead of
extrapolating them. Writing the sigma-form ODE from memory would violate
HARD RULE 1, and accepting it from the operator is the same violation one
channel removed. So it was discovered instead.

L-036 VERIFIED. sigma = s(log det)' with derivatives taken analytically from
the Nystrom matrix (validated against central differences at 5.4e-19).
Nullspace over 70 monomials of degree <=4 at 82 sample points. First attempt
failed informatively: no spectral gap, 34 spurious null directions from
conditioning. After row/column equilibration and a narrow window, ONE null
direction at 1e-122 with a 45-order gap. Six exact dyadic coefficients out of
70; the other 64 at noise:

  s^2 sigma''^2 + 16 u^2 + 4 u sigma'^2 = 0,   u = s sigma' - sigma

Confirmed out of sample at s=6..30 (window was s in [1,4]) to 79 digits,
with a control validated against its own resolution first.

L-037 PROVEN (conditional on L-036). Triangular recursion for the tail, no
parity assumed. All odd orders vanish identically to m=400, so the even-only
model fit_constant.py has assumed since revision 1 is now derived rather than
fitted. e_2 = 1/32 exactly, settling IQ-2 by derivation. Two independent
implementations (sympy; exact Fractions) agree on all 30 shared orders with
zero mismatches. 200 orders in 70 s.

L-038 VERIFIED. c read off as the integration constant after exact tail
subtraction: no design matrix, no extrapolation, no conditioning wall, no E1.
Optimal truncation predicted at m* ~ 2s and measured at M*=296 for s=149.
132.09 honest digits, agreeing with (1/12)log2 + 3 zeta'(-1) to 131.81.
Error bar calibrated by three cross-s checks, all landing at ~80% of the
predicted bar. Previous best was 73 digits from 224 points and ~4 h.

The honest accounting: ~6 h of grid compute in this project bought 2 digits;
70 s of recursion bought 59. Every point purchased after revision 3 addressed
a budget line that was not binding.
